# Brainrot Blitz — a 3D brainrot tycoon

**Play it:** https://www.roblox.com/games/89773665418299

A bright 3D plaza on Roblox where absurd meme characters walk a red-carpet
conveyor. Walk up, click one to buy it with coins, and it walks to your base
and earns $/sec. Sixteen original characters across six rarities — Common,
Rare, Epic, Legendary, Mythic, Secret — from Sir Quackington the duck in a
top hat ($5/s) to The Infinite Spaghetti Monster ($1,200/s), with Golden,
Diamond, Rainbow and Blood mutations multiplying their income.

Then the tension: click a rival's brainrot to steal it and carry it home on
your back (slower while carrying) — unless their base lock is on. Lock your
base for 60 seconds, slap thieves to make them drop their catch, and when
the tsunami warning sounds, run home: the giant water wall sweeps the plaza
every 120 seconds, knocking back everyone caught outside their base and
dropping 10% of their coins. Rebirth at $10,000 for a permanent +25%
income multiplier and +2 base slots. Golden Hour doubles everyone's income
for 60 seconds every few minutes, and every Mythic spawn triggers its own
server-wide 2x window.

Built with Rojo (Luau modules under `src/`, one job each) and handed in by
`main.ts`.

## Run

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

`rojo build default.project.json --output game.rbxl` rebuilds the place
from `src/` first.
